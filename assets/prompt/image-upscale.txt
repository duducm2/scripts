Partial cleanup is a failure. If even one word, one letter, or one icon stroke still has a grayish blur, color fringe, or muddy edge at 200% zoom, the output is wrong. Redo until every edge is clean.

Common defects you must eliminate completely:

1. Gray or light halo ringing black/dark text on white or light backgrounds.
2. Purple, magenta, cyan, or green color fringing on letter or icon edges (compression/subpixel bleed).
3. Mosquito noise, blocky patches, or mottling in white or flat-color areas near edges.
4. Soft, feathered, or bicubic-smoothed strokes on text, lines, curves, and icons.
5. Compression ringing (faint repeating shadow) around high-contrast edges.
6. Jagged low-res pixels that look enlarged instead of reconstructed.

Wrong: any visible gray band between ink and background; dirty white space; fuzzy curves on icons.
Right: vector-export quality — crisp boundary, uniform solid fill inside the stroke, uniform solid background outside, with no intermediate blur zone.
