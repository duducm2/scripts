## Procedure — SMASH mode

1. Each merge group → one smashed beast after re-sequencing. Pegs not listed stay single-atom.
2. Max 4 pegs per merge group; split larger groups and note splits in the chat note.
3. Schema blending / Anatomical Micro-Loci: zones Z1→Z4; distinct sensory channels; neutral beast body (`is_smashed=1`); each sub-atom has its own concept + quote preserved; story encodes zone beats.
4. Re-peg A, B, C, …; collapse empty palaces; max 5 beasts per palace; one character per palace.

## Procedure — REMOVE mode

1. Remove atoms whose **current** peg is listed.
2. Bestiary shifting for remaining beasts; update beast names/pegs.
3. Rewrite palace/beast/atom rows; preserve quotes; keep smashed structure when survivors stay zoned.
