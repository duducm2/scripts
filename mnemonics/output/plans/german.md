## Phase 1: Basic User (A1 – A2) – Foundation and Routine Communication

### Months 1–3: CEFR Level A1 (Absolute Beginner)

#### 1. Phonetics and Pronunciation Foundation

- [ ] ⚡ Entender as diferenças do r no alemão
- [ ] Memorize the German alphabet and its phonetic pronunciation.
- [ ] Master the articulatory requirements of the Umlauts (ä, ö, ü).
- [ ] Practice the distinct fricative sounds of 'ch' (ich-Laut vs. ach-Laut) and 'sch'.
- [ ] Recognize and produce the difference between long and short vowels.
- [ ] Master Terminal Devoicing (Auslautverhärtung): ending 'b', 'd', 'g' sound like 'p', 't', 'k'.
- [ ] Practice the Glottal Stop (Knacklaut) at word/syllable boundaries.
- [ ] Learn R-vocalization (vokalisches R): turning ending 'r' into a short 'a' sound (e.g., Vater).

**🔗 Resources:**

- 🔍 [Deutsch für Euch: German Pronunciation & Alphabet](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Deutsch+f%C3%BCr+Euch+Pronunciation)
- 🔍 [Fluent Forever: German Pronunciation - Vowels and Consonants](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Fluent+Forever+German+Pronunciation)

#### 2. Core Grammar: Sentence Structure & Verbs

- [ ] Master Subject-Verb-Object (SVO) declarative sentence structure.
- [ ] Formulate interrogatives using W-words (wer, was, wann, wo, warum, wie).
- [ ] Conjugate regular and irregular verbs in the present tense (Präsens).
- [ ] Conjugate foundational modal verbs (können, müssen, wollen, dürfen).
- [ ] Understand Separable and Inseparable verbs (Trennbare und Untrennbare Verben).
- [ ] Form commands using the Imperative mood (Imperativ).

**🔗 Resources:**

- 🔍 [Lingoni German (Jenny): Complete A1 Playlist](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Lingoni+German+A1+Playlist)
- 🔍 [Learn German with Anja: Full A1 Course](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Learn+German+with+Anja+Full+A1+Course)

#### 3. Core Grammar: Nouns, Cases & Negation

- [ ] Memorize grammatical gender classifications: masculine (der), feminine (die), neuter (das).
- [ ] Learn standard plural formation patterns.
- [ ] Distinguish between Nominative (subject) and Accusative (direct object) cases.
- [ ] Modify definite, indefinite, and possessive articles (mein, dein, sein...) for Nominative and Accusative.
- [ ] Master the syntax of negation: _nicht_ (for verbs/adjectives) vs. _kein_ (for nouns).

**🔗 Resources:**

#### 4. Vocabulary Acquisition (500–650 Words)

- [ ] Personal introductions and family structures.
- [ ] Immediate surroundings and daily routines.
- [ ] Basic shopping interactions, food, and drink.
- [ ] Numerical fluency and calendar dates.

**🔗 Resources:**

- 🔍 [Natürlich German: A1 Comprehensible Input (Storytelling)](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Naturlich+German+A1)

#### 5. Listening, Reading, and Writing

- [ ] Listen to slow, clearly articulated speech (predictable phrasing).
- [ ] Read short, highly contextualized texts (signs, menus, basic emails).
- [ ] Write standard forms with personal details and short functional messages.

**🔗 Resources:**

- **Video:** [Easy German: Super Easy German (Absolute Beginners)](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Super+Easy+German)

- [ ] Take a free online A1 mock exam (Goethe-Institut or telc website).

### Months 4–6: CEFR Level A2 (Advanced Beginner)

#### 1. Advanced Syntax: Subordinate Clauses & Word Order

- [ ] Integrate subordinating conjunctions (weil, dass, obwohl, wenn).
- [ ] Practice moving the conjugated verb to the absolute end of the subordinate clause.
- [ ] Master the TeKaMoLo rule for sentence word order (Temporal, Kausal, Modal, Lokal).

**🔗 Resources:**

- 🔍 [Learn German with Anja: Subordinate Clauses (dass, weil, wenn)](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Learn+German+with+Anja+Subordinate+Clauses)

#### 2. Temporal Scope: Past Tenses

- [ ] Master the Present Perfect (Perfekt) for spoken communication.
- [ ] Memorize past participles and auxiliary verb selection (haben vs. sein).
- [ ] Introduce the Simple Past (Präteritum) for modal and auxiliary verbs.

**🔗 Resources:**

- 🔍 [YourGermanTeacher: The Perfekt Tense in German](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=YourGermanTeacher+Perfekt+Tense)

#### 3. Morphological Complexity: Dative, Genitive & Adjectives

- [ ] Learn strictly dative verbs and declension of dative personal pronouns.
- [ ] Master two-way prepositions (Wechselpräpositionen) for Accusative vs. Dative.
- [ ] Introduce the Genitive case (Genitiv) for expressing possession.
- [ ] Understand the n-declension (n-Deklination) for specific masculine nouns.
- [ ] Introduce foundational adjective declensions based on gender, number, and case.
- [ ] Form and use the Comparative and Superlative degrees of adjectives.
- [ ] Master Reflexive verbs and pronouns (e.g., sich interessieren).

#### 4. Vocabulary (up to 1,500 Words), Speaking & Early Culture

- [ ] Expand vocabulary to professional backgrounds, housing, and leisure.
- [ ] Introduce basic DACH regional distinctions (Germany, Austria, Switzerland cultural norms).
- [ ] Practice sentence-level intonation and connected speech rhythms.
- [ ] **Live Interaction Milestone:** Schedule a 30-minute language exchange (via Tandem or HelloTalk) once a week to practice basic, unscripted dialogues.

## Phase 2: Independent User (B1 – B2) – Intermediate Fluency and Expression

### Months 7–10: CEFR Level B1 (Intermediate)

#### 1. Syntactical Depth & Cohesion

- [ ] Master Relative Clauses (Relativsätze) with properly declined relative pronouns.
- [ ] Learn the Passive Voice (Passiv) in present and simple past tenses.
- [ ] Use Subjunctive II (Konjunktiv II) for polite requests and hypothetical situations (ich würde, ich hätte, ich wäre).
- [ ] Form Infinitive clauses (Infinitiv mit zu, um... zu, ohne... zu, anstatt... zu).

**🔗 Resources:**

- 🔍 [YourGermanTeacher: B1 Grammar - Relative Clauses & Konjunktiv II](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=YourGermanTeacher+B1+Relative+Clauses+Konjunktiv+II)

#### 2. Advanced Verbs, Tenses & Adjectives

- [ ] Memorize verbs with fixed prepositions (Verben mit Präpositionen, e.g., warten auf).
- [ ] Use Da-compounds and Wo-compounds (darauf, worauf, damit, womit) to reference ideas/objects.
- [ ] Introduce the Past Perfect (Plusquamperfekt) for sequencing past events.
- [ ] Learn the Future Tense (Futur I) using _werden_.
- [ ] Automate adjective declensions across Nominative, Accusative, and Dative cases.

**🔗 Resources:**

## Autonomous Execution Framework: Daily and Weekly Protocols

### Daily Micro-Routine (1.5 to 2.5 Hours)

#### Morning: Lexical Anchoring (20–30 Minutes)

- [ ] Review 10–20 new words using Spaced Repetition Software (Anki or Quizlet).
- [ ] Vocalize the word, state its definite article (der/die/das), plural form, and generate a contextualized sample sentence.

#### Afternoon: Comprehensible Input & Phonetic Mimicry (30–45 Minutes)

- [ ] Consume passive and active listening content appropriate for your CEFR level.
- [ ] **Active Shadowing:** Select a 2-minute segment. Listen, pause, and repeat aloud, mimicking intonation, rhythm, and articulation perfectly.
- [ ] **Optional (A1–A2):** Use [Slow German](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Slow+German+Annik+Rubens) for shadowing: read the transcript from [slowgerman.com](https://slowgerman.com/), then listen and repeat phrase by phrase.

#### Evening: Syntactic Construction & Output Generation (30–45 Minutes)

- [ ] Watch one highly specific grammar instructional video.
- [ ] Write 5 to 10 original, complex sentences physically applying the newly learned grammatical rule.
- [ ] **Active Feedback Loop:** Instantly validate your sentences using AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT/Claude) instructed to act as a strict German grammar teacher. Submit longer paragraphs weekly to native speakers on LangCorrect or to your tutor to prevent fossilizing errors.

### Weekly Macro-Routine (Thematic Cycles)

- [ ] **Monday – Wednesday (Acquisition):** Focus entirely on ingesting new structural grammar rules and thematic vocabulary clusters.
- [ ] **Thursday – Friday (Application & Correction):** Transition to output. Write short essays, emails, or record audio diaries. **Mandatory:** Submit this output for external correction.
- [ ] **Saturday (Simulation & Live Interaction):**
- [ ] **Sunday (Passive Recovery):** Discard active study. Consume German media (podcasts, vlogs, music, films) purely for entertainment to allow subconscious consolidation.
