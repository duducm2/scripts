# 📚 Research Tools Reference

A curated list of tools for literature review, citation mapping, and access to scientific knowledge.

## 1. ResearchRabbit

**Purpose:** Visual Literature Discovery

* **Description:** Often called the "Spotify for Papers," it builds visual collections of research. It learns from your inputs to recommend relevant papers and visualizes the network of citations and authors.
* **Best for:** Finding papers similar to ones you already love and visualizing author networks.
* **Key Feature:** Interactive citation maps and email alerts for new relevant papers.

## 2. Inciteful.xyz

**Purpose:** Citation Network Analysis

* **Description:** Uses citation graphs to find the "missing link" papers in your research. It helps identify seminal papers and the connections between two different papers (the "shortest path").
* **Best for:** Deep diving into a specific topic and ensuring you haven't missed fundamental studies.
* **Key Feature:** "Graph Search" to explore the ecosystem around a specific paper.

## 3. Sci-Hub

**Purpose:** Universal Access to Knowledge

* **Description:** A shadow library website that provides free access to millions of research papers and books, bypassing publisher paywalls.
* **Best for:** Retrieving full-text PDFs of articles that are otherwise behind a paywall.
* **Key Feature:** Direct PDF downloads via DOI or URL.
* *Note: Operates in a legal gray area in many jurisdictions.*

## 4. ScholarAI

**Purpose:** AI-Powered Research Assistant

* **Description:** An AI tool (often integrated as a plugin for LLMs like ChatGPT) designed to search, analyze, and summarize scientific literature reliably. It connects directly to peer-reviewed databases to reduce hallucinations.
* **Best for:** Extracting key takeaways, methodologies, and data from papers quickly using natural language.
* **Key Feature:** Direct queries against a database of open-access papers.

## 5. Semantic Scholar

**Purpose:** AI-Driven Search Engine

* **Description:** A free, AI-backed search engine for scientific literature. It understands the context of citations (e.g., whether a paper supports or disputes a claim) rather than just keyword matching.
* **Best for:** Initial broad searches and filtering results by field, date, and influence.
* **Key Feature:** "Highly Influential Citations" filtering and "TL;DR" (Too Long; Didn't Read) AI summaries for papers.
* **Link:** [semanticscholar.org](https://www.semanticscholar.org/)
