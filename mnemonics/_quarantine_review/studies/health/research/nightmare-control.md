# Nightmare control

## **Evening (IRT - Imagery Rehearsal Therapy)**

🔲 Before IRT: calm body with paced breathing or progressive muscle relaxation

🔲 Pick one moderate recurring nightmare first (avoid the worst trauma replay at the start)

🔲 Write the nightmare in full detail (senses, thoughts, emotions)

🔲 Rescript the story before the climax: new ending = safety, mastery, or peace (no forced family reconciliation in the dream)

🔲 Rehearse the rescripted dream 10–20 minutes daily, ideally before sleep

🔲 If stuck: use a boundary, barrier, escape, or absurd mastery (e.g. shrink threat) — not apology or reunion with unsafe people

## **Optional — TMR (Targeted Memory Reactivation) (if you use sleep tech)**

🔲 Pair the same neutral sound with daytime rehearsal of the rescripted dream

🔲 Play that same cue softly during REM (Rapid Eye Movement) sleep (per device instructions)

## **Pre-sleep**

🔲 Extended-exhale breathing (e.g. inhale ~4, exhale longer ~8)

🔲 Hum, chant, sing, or gargle with water ~2 minutes

🔲 Brief cold-water face immersion (forehead, eyes, cheeks) for dive reflex

🔲 Gentle foot massage or neck stretches / pressure

🔲 Run one mentalization exercise: Dream Journey, My Boundaries, or Open Thermometer (skip heavy rumination when exhausted)

## **If you wake from a nightmare**

🔲 Visual orienting: slowly look around and name neutral objects out loud

🔲 Butterfly hug: alternate gentle taps on shoulders for several minutes

🔲 Shake out arms, legs, and shoulders for 30–60 seconds

🔲 Press feet into the floor; shift weight side to side; feel contact

## **Morning / environment**

🔲 Use sunrise / gradual light 30–60 minutes before wake time (or bright light on waking)

🔲 Keep water by the bed; drink a large glass on waking

🔲 Get out of bed immediately (no lingering rumination in bed)

🔲 Thinking window: at most 20 minutes for nightmare / conflict journaling or thought — then stop and move on

🔲 Low-capacity day: do only the minimum BA (Behavioral Activation) (water, light, out of bed)

🔲 Normal day: walk ~5 minutes indoors; gentle neck and ankle rotations; ~10 minutes restorative stretching

🔲 Make bed; shower and change out of sleepwear; eat a planned breakfast

🔲 ~10 minutes pleasure or mastery (hobby, plant, podcast) or text an accountability partner

## **Ongoing**

🔲 Remember: forgiveness is internal; reconciliation requires mutual safety — not the same thing

🔲 Self-compassion: validate hurt without shaming yourself for anger, grief, or distance
