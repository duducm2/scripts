Linguists

https://youtube.com/@pbsstoried?si=sBROFskPXAGtJGcK

- [ ] 🟧 3Blue1Brown  
       Created by Grant Sanderson, this channel is the gold standard for visualizing complex mathematics. It is an absolute must-watch for understanding the linear algebra, calculus, and abstract algebra that power Neural Networks and Machine Learning.

- [ ] 🟧 Two Minute Papers  
       Hosted by Károly Zsolnai Fejér, this channel is essential viewing for keeping up with the rapid pace of artificial intelligence. It breaks down the latest academic research papers in Machine Learning, computer graphics, and physics simulations.

- [ ] 🟧 Kurzgesagt – In a Nutshell  
       Renowned for breathtaking vector animations and data visualization. They tackle existential scientific questions, biology, and astrophysics with rigorous academic fact checking.

- [ ] 🟧 Veritasium  
       Hosted by Derek Muller, this channel excels at debunking scientific misconceptions. It covers quantum physics, engineering, and the history of scientific discovery, often testing hypotheses with real world experiments.

- [ ] 🟧 Computerphile and Numberphile  
       Sister channels featuring unscripted interviews with university professors. They dive deep into the beauty of abstract mathematics, cryptography, data structures, algorithms, and programming logic.

- [ ] 🟧 PBS Space Time  
       Hosted by astrophysicist Matt O'Dowd, this is one of the most advanced science channels on the platform. It offers graduate level explorations of quantum mechanics, general relativity, and theoretical physics.

- [ ] 🟧 Tom Scott  
       A massive archive exploring computer science, geography, and linguistics. He excels at breaking down edge cases in programming, web architecture, and the intricacies of human language and phonetics.

- [ ] 🟧 Vsauce  
       Michael Stevens takes seemingly simple or absurd questions and spirals them into profound explorations of mathematics, psychology, and physical laws.

- [ ] 🟧 Smarter Every Day  
       Aerospace engineer Destin Sandlin uses high speed cameras and physical experiments to explore fluid dynamics, mechanical engineering, and optical physics.

- [ ] 🟧 Mark Rober  
       A former NASA engineer who builds incredibly complex mechanical engineering projects, teaching the scientific method, iterative design, and coding along the way.
