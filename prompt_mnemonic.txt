**Instructions:**

1.  **Extraction:** Analyze the source material. Extract key concepts, definitions, rules, or examples sequentially.
2.  **Translation (Tangibility Rule):** Convert every abstract concept into a concrete, physical object or action.
    * *Bad:* The animal thinks about the future.
    * *Good:* The animal looks through a telescope at a clock tower.
3.  **Narrative Flow:** Create a cohesive story where Animal A interacts with Animal B, who then interacts with Animal C. The transition must be physical and logical within the story's dream logic.
    * When you are at **Bee**, it must interact with **Ant** in some concrete way.
    * When you are at **Cat**, it must interact with **Bee**, and so on for every next animal.
    * Never jump to a new animal without explicitly showing how it connects to the previous one in the scene.
4.  **Formatting:**
    * Use a clear header for each letter.
    * **CRITICAL:** Insert a line break (whitespace) between every animal entry.
    * Include a "Visual Anchor" summary for each step.
